01 / Profile

About關於我

Wei-Ping Li is a critical care registered nurse and PhD student in biomedical informatics whose clinical and research interests span critical care, cardiovascular intensive care, biomedical informatics, clinical artificial intelligence, and aeromedical evacuation.

李偉平為重症護理師及生物醫學資訊博士生，臨床與研究領域涵蓋重症醫療、心臟血管加護照護、生物醫學資訊、臨床人工智慧及空中傷患後送。

His professional background combines frontline critical care practice with interdisciplinary research in healthcare quality, clinical informatics, predictive modeling, and data-driven decision support.

其專業背景結合第一線重症臨床實務與跨領域研究，關注醫療品質、臨床資訊、預測模型及資料驅動之臨床決策支援。
