01 / Profile

About 關於我

Wei-Ping Li is a clinician-researcher whose professional interests connect critical care nursing, aeromedical evacuation, military medicine, biomedical informatics, and clinical artificial intelligence. 李偉平為臨床護理與生物醫學資訊領域研究者， 專業與研究興趣橫跨重症護理、航空後送、 軍事醫療、生物醫學資訊與臨床人工智慧。

His research focuses on translating clinical data into interpretable and clinically useful decision-support tools, with particular interests in predictive modeling, survival analysis, machine learning, and critical care analytics. 研究重點聚焦於如何將臨床資料轉化為具有可解釋性、 且能實際支援臨床決策的工具，涵蓋預測模型、 生存分析、機器學習及重症資料分析。
